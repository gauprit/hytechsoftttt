GST & Invoicing Knowledge Base

How do I calculate GST for Service Exports from India?

▼

Service exports are generally treated as Zero-Rated Supplies. You can export services under an LUT (Letter of Undertaking) without paying IGST, or pay IGST and claim a refund later. Use the Hytechsoft GST tool to verify your base amounts before filing.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

▼

The type of tax depends on the location of the supplier and the recipient:

  • Intra-state (Same State): CGST + SGST are applied (e.g., 9% each for an 18% slab).
  • Inter-state (Different States): Only IGST is applied (18% total).
